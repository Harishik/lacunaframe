# The reconstruction desk

The interface treats video recovery as an editing and measurement task. The workspace has a source bin, a large synchronized frame viewer, and a right-hand experiment inspector. The frame strip and sample-availability track share the same index as the comparison, error map, and measured frame error. Missing observations have explicit labels.

Graphite surfaces keep attention on the footage. Warm paper and a restrained serif distinguish the project narrative from the working instrument. Coral marks damage/actions; blue marks reconstruction/error. Instrument Sans handles controls and readable prose; IBM Plex Mono handles timecodes and measurements. Fonts are bundled locally. The LF mark is a small, independently drawn SVG inspired by an interrupted frame.

Workspace, Evaluation, and The project answer separate questions: what am I changing, what actually happened, and why was this built? Controls perform real actions. There are no decorative metrics, simulated processing bars, stock dashboards, generated imagery, or unrelated hero illustrations. All photographic media comes from the attributed DAVIS sequences; output previews come from recorded restoration runs.

On smaller screens, the source bin becomes a drawer and settings follow the viewer. Keyboard controls, form labels, a skip link, visible focus, a native help dialog, explicit offline states, and chart-frame selection support non-pointer use. Automated Axe WCAG A/AA checks supplement browser interaction tests; they do not replace a full assistive-technology audit.

## Documentation as a companion experience

The visual README and `docs/experience/index.html` extend the reconstruction desk into a guided reading route. The guide runs from local files with embedded measured data, a real-frame wipe, corruption-regime chart controls, disclosures, and copyable commands. It respects reduced motion and prints with a static alternative to the GIF. Figures are generated by `scripts/build_documentation.py` from actual arrays; the documentation checker validates chart provenance and frame selection. Browser captures `07-documentation.png` and `08-documentation-mobile.png` show the rendered guide.
