# Protocol v1: recoverability-aware missing-frame recovery (design freeze)

Frozen 2026-11-20, **before** any new data is downloaded or any new method is tuned. Changing this file after results exist requires a dated amendment at the bottom that gives the reason. Machine-readable copy: [recoverability-v1.json](recoverability-v1.json).

## 1. Questions

- **Q1 (C1):** How do reconstruction methods degrade with gap length and loss process on real footage?
- **Q2 (C2):** Can expected reconstruction error be predicted from observations alone, before reconstructing, per frame and per pixel, and does the prediction transfer across reconstructors and datasets?
- **Q3 (C3):** Does conditioning conformal risk control on that prediction give tighter risk masks at equal guaranteed coverage, and does coverage survive shifts in gap length and loss process?

## 2. Storage budget (hard cap)

| Item | Cap |
|---|---|
| All external data + model weights for this project | **6 GB total** |
| Any single dataset after extraction | 1.5 GB |
| Working set of derived arrays | 3 GB |

Rules: test or validation splits only, never training sets. Use **curated clip subsets**, never whole datasets. Prefer per-file downloads over monolithic archives. Delete archives and raw frames once derived lossless arrays are written. Derived arrays are 640×360 (or a centre crop at native scale for high-motion sets), uint8 `.npz`. Data lives under `$LACUNA_EXTERNAL` (default `~/lacuna-data`), outside git. Every download is listed with source, size and running total in `research/data/LEDGER.md` *before* it happens, and the owner approves the list.

## 3. Data and splits

- **Sources (candidates, sizes to be verified under the cap):** subsets of Vimeo90K septuplet test, X4K1000FPS test, SNU-FILM hard/extreme, Xiph 2K/4K, MA-HD if released. DAVIS stays **diagnostic only** (already used, possible pretraining overlap).
- **Split by clip identity, frozen before tuning:** *dev* (feature design, conformal calibration) and *test* (touched once per final configuration). Test clips come from datasets *not* used for dev wherever possible, which gives the cross-dataset shift test.
- Pretraining overlap is recorded per model and dataset; it's never assumed absent.

## 4. Tasks and loss processes

| Task | Definition |
|---|---|
| T1 midpoint | Two anchors, t = 0.5 (comparability with the VFI literature) |
| T2 arbitrary time | Two anchors, t ∈ {1/8, …, 7/8} |
| T3 missing runs | Contiguous gaps of g ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32} frames inside longer clips |

T3 losses are generated by three seeded processes: **Bernoulli** (i.i.d. drops), **Gilbert–Elliott** burst loss (two-state Markov, matched mean loss rate), and **GOP-chain loss** (losing a reference frame invalidates its dependents until the next I-frame). Seeds are fixed per clip. Solvers see observed frames and missing flags only.

## 5. Methods

Controls: hold, linear, two-anchor Farnebäck flow. Multi-anchor: quadratic-motion flow using two observed frames on each side of the gap (new in v1). Learned: RIFE 4.9 (pinned). Later baselines, each first parity-checked against its own published numbers: IFRNet, AMT, EMA-VFI, FILM, BiM-VFI, one diffusion VFI. Recoverability baseline: a VFI-DPA-style pair-difficulty regressor trained on the same dev data.

## 6. Metrics

- **Fidelity:** RGB PSNR and SSIM on affected pixels (current conventions), plus each baseline's own official convention when doing parity checks. LPIPS and DISTS are added once their weights are approved under the cap.
- **Temporal:** the existing temporal-difference error; flow-warping error once a reference flow is available.
- **C2:** Spearman ρ and Kendall τ between predicted and actual error (per frame and per pixel), and AUROC for "error > τ".
- **C3:** empirical risk vs the target α, mean flagged-area fraction, and coverage stratified by gap length and loss process.

## 7. Statistics

The scene (clip) is the unit of analysis. Report scene-level means with **95% bootstrap CIs over scenes** (10,000 resamples), and **paired** method differences with a scene-level bootstrap CI and a Wilcoxon signed-rank test, Holm-corrected across comparisons. Frames and seeds are never treated as independent samples. A claim of "better" requires the paired CI to exclude 0 on the test split.

## 8. Pre-registered success and kill gates

| Gate | Pass condition (test split) | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| G1 multi-anchor | Paired ΔPSNR vs two-anchor flow > 0 with CI excluding 0 for g ≥ 4 | Report as a negative result and keep two-anchor flow |
| G2 recoverability | Per-frame Spearman ρ ≥ 0.6 on held-out datasets, beating the VFI-DPA-style baseline with a paired CI excluding 0 | C2 can't be a headline; lead with C1 |
| G3 calibration | Coverage within ±2 points of 1−α on every gap and loss stratum | Diagnose with weighted/Mondrian CP before any claim |
| G4 conditioning | Conditioned masks ≥ 15% smaller flagged area than unconditioned CRC at equal coverage | C3 becomes an application, not a contribution |

## 9. Reproducibility

Pinned FFmpeg decoding (`backend/decode.py`), checksums for every source and checkpoint, seeded corruption, saved predictions, all failures retained, one command per table.
